# 9. Finance & Reporting

## Finance

Finance functionality added in the final upgrade work covers:

- Expense tracking
- Asset-linked expenses
- Supplier-linked expenses
- Budgets
- Monthly/yearly spend
- Budget remaining
- Financial audit information

## Reporting

Reporting and analytics cover areas including:

- Asset values
- Asset status/lifecycle
- Location/category analysis
- Maintenance spend
- Expense spend
- Management KPIs
- CSV export

## Executive Centre

The executive/management area combines operational indicators into a management view.

Numbers should be interpreted according to their underlying records and reporting period. Financial figures should be reconciled against the source records before external use.
